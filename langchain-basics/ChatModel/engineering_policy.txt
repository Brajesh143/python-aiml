ENGINEERING DEPARTMENT POLICY

The Engineering department follows a flexible work policy.

Working Hours:
Engineering employees normally work from 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM, Monday to Friday.
Employees can adjust their working hours with prior approval from their manager.

Leave Policy:
Engineering employees receive 20 days of paid leave per year.
Employees should submit leave requests at least 3 days in advance.
Emergency leave can be requested without the 3-day notice period.

Work From Home:
Engineering employees can work from home up to 2 days per week.
Additional remote work requires manager approval.

Performance Review:
Engineering employees receive a performance review twice a year.
Performance reviews are conducted by the employee's manager and department lead.

Learning and Development:
Engineering employees can request a learning budget of up to INR 30,000 per year.
The training or certification should be relevant to the employee's current role or career development.

Security Policy:
Employees must not share company source code, credentials, API keys, or confidential documents with unauthorized people.

The Engineering department is responsible for maintaining application code, APIs, cloud infrastructure, and internal engineering tools.
